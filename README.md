# Intermaven Platform

Unified AI Business, Smart CRM, Social AI, Hospitality & Creative Ecosystem — connected via single sign-on and shared MongoDB infrastructure with **TuneMavens**, **TuneStream**, and **SyncMavens**.

## Local Development Ports
- **Intermaven Frontend**: `http://localhost:3005` (`frontend/`, React 18)
- **Intermaven Backend**: `http://localhost:8005` (`backend/`, FastAPI + MongoDB — 33 modular routers)
- **TuneMavens Frontend / Backend**: `http://localhost:3000` / `http://localhost:8001`
- **TuneStream / SyncMavens**: `http://localhost:3001` / `http://localhost:3002`

## Platform Highlights (November 2026)

### 1. 22 Reconciled Marketplace & Studio Applications
- **AI Creative & Business Studio**: Brand Kit AI, Music Bio & EPK Builder, Social AI, Sync Pitch AI, Pitch Deck AI, **Press Release AI**, **Lyric & Hook AI**, **Merch Designer Brief AI**, and **AI Output Library** (`db.ai_outputs` auto-archive with search & favorites).
- **Business, Legal & Finance Suite**: **Smart CRM & Reachout** (53-route persona playbook & campaign engine), **Business Contract Builder** (`NDA`, `Work-for-Hire`, `Collaboration`, `Client Service` templates + e-signature), **Invoice & Payments**, **M-Pesa & Card POS Terminal**, and **Hosting & Domain Manager**.
- **Music, Events & Growth Suite**: **Distribution Tracker** (181 global DSP outlets), **Tour Manager** (integrated with the **TuneMavens Events Network** + VIP guest lists + live EPK `events_upcoming` sync), **Royalty Calculator**, **Content Calendar**, **Affiliates Program**, and **File Manager** (S3 / Cloudflare R2 ready).
- **Grant Finder, Submission & Pipeline Tracker**: 10 curated African & global funding programs with **Maven AI Auto-Prefill** (synthesizing User Profile, Ayo Business Discovery, Brand Kit AI, and Artist EPK with funder requirements & timelines), **Direct Email Packet Dispatch & Assisted Portal Tracking**, **6-Stage Pipeline Tracker** (`Draft` → `Submitted` → `Under Review` → `Shortlisted` → `Approved` / `Declined`), and a **Multi-Channel Communications Hub** (Email, WhatsApp, SMS, Inbound Funder Reply logging, and 1-click Maven AI follow-up templates).

### 2. Mother-CMS Control Center (`/cms` & Admin Studio)
- **69 Portal-Aware & Region-Aware Keys**: Distinct content resolution across **Global**, **Business**, **Music (TuneMavens)**, and **Hospitality** portals and **7 regional targets** (`DEFAULT`, `KE`, `US`, `GB`, `CA`, `NG`, `ZA`).
- **Interactive Live Hero Preview & AI Copywriter**: Real-time 4-slide hero preview, rich-text formatting toolbar, `/api/ai/cms-assist` copywriter, Site Media Asset Library, and full revision history with 1-click rollback.

### 3. Phase 9 Admin SEO Management Module (`/admin/seo`)
- **Global & Per-Route SEO Engine**: Title templates, 18 seeded route overrides, Google Search Console & Bing Webmaster verification tags, local-safe **Open Graph / X Card & Google SERP Live Previews**, and 1-click **AI Suggest Meta**.
- **Dynamic Sitemap, Robots & JSON-LD `@graph`**: Live `/sitemap.xml` (auto-including published artist EPKs), `/robots.txt`, Organization + `sameAs` structured data, and GDPR / Kenya DPA consent-gated pixels (`GA4`, `GTM`, `Meta`, `TikTok`, `LinkedIn`, `PostHog`, `Plausible`).
- **Automated SEO Audit & 1-Click Remediation**: Verifies meta lengths, duplicates, JSX `<img alt>` coverage (`100%`), and internal link integrity (`100%`) with in-system `Auto-Fix All` and inline token configuration (`100/100` verified score).

### 4. Sentinel Cross-Platform QA Agent & Hardened Auth
- **Sentinel QA (`/api/admin/test-agent`)**: Portable diagnostic runner executing health, auth, CMS, SEO, Distro, Events, Grants, and Calendar checks across `intermaven`, `tunemavens`, `tunestream`, and `syncmavens` with AI triage summaries.
- **Self-Serve Auth & Pinned Sidebar Dock**: SHA-256 single-use Forgot/Reset/Change Password flows, 1-click Sandbox Login (`8005`), collapsible `12px` sidebar navigation with independent scrolling, and a permanently visible bottom dock for `Mother-CMS`, `SEO Manager`, `Admin Console`, `Settings`, and `Log Out`.
